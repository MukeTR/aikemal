# AI Kemal — English

> AI Kemal is the digital workshop of Mustafa Kemal Karataş: founder, AI product builder and the person who tends to turn “what if?” into a working repository.

Mustafa Kemal is the founder and solopreneur builder of Kârmatik and Independent AI, and co-founder of Dipixel Media. He works across AI, SaaS, e-commerce, CRM, automation, competitive strategy, web research, data extraction and Meta advertising. Infrastructure and model choices are made around the real traffic, data, operating and cost constraints of each product.

## Formative story

Curiosity came before the job titles. At 12, Mustafa Kemal used dictionaries and Google Translate to work through Portuguese instructions on a Brazilian website and jailbreak the PlayStation 3 his family could not afford games for. One or two years later, that skill led to his first job at Koçer Elektronik in Istanbul's Yazıcıoğlu Business Centre.

In his second year of high school, his father enrolled him at BilgeAdam Academy. During an intensive two-year programme, self-taught experimentation became software, architecture and project discipline. Before graduating from high school, he was already taking real responsibility on ASP.NET projects alongside graduates of some of Turkey's leading universities and members of the country's early generation of software professionals.

The same pattern continued in customer-facing work: listen closely, find the real problem behind the visible complaint, recognise the pattern and turn it into a working system.

## Working style

- AI is a working habit, not a decorative slide.
- Find the real competitor before writing the strategy.
- Find the right source before collecting the data.
- Choose infrastructure for the product, not for fashion.
- Keep fixed cost low and the working output high.
- Listen closely: good products often begin as one sentence someone actually heard.
- “Impossible” is often a problem that has not been broken into small enough pieces.
